The practice loop

Everything a real panel round has, minus the stakes.

📄

Résumé + JD aware

Drop in your résumé and the job posting. Questions are generated from the overlap and the gaps between them — not a generic bank.

🎙️

Actually spoken aloud

Camera on, mic on, Ava asks out loud and you answer out loud — with a typed fallback any time your browser or environment can't do audio.

♻️

Never repeats a question

Every session tracks what you've already been asked, so a second practice run digs into new territory instead of retreading the last one.

📊

Scored, not vibes

Filler-word rate, answer depth, relevance to the JD, and pacing roll up into one confidence score with the specific moments that pulled it down.

🕘

A history that compounds

Every rehearsal is logged. Watch your score trend, and see the exact question you fumbled last Tuesday before you walk into Friday's interview.

🔒

Stays on your device

Résumés, answers, and scores are stored locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a third-party server.

How a session runs

Four steps, about twenty minutes.

01

Upload résumé & JD

Paste or drop the two documents Aimock will build the interview from.

02

Set the format

Pick role focus, difficulty, and how many questions — technical, behavioral, or mixed.

03

Answer out loud

Camera and mic on, Ava asks each question and listens live, no repeats, no rehearsed order.

04

Get the breakdown

A scored report lands instantly, with the exact lines that need work — saved to your history.
